## Set up in about 10 minutes

### Have these ready

- Your **EIN** (or SSN if you're a sole proprietor)
- Your **business bank account** and routing numbers, or your online-banking login
- A **driver's license** or other photo ID for the owner
- Your business **address and phone**

### Steps

1. **Open your setup link:** {{SETUP_URL}}
   It's personal to your business. If it ever says it's expired, call us for a new one.
2. **Create or sign in to your Stripe account** with your business email.
3. **Answer Stripe's questions** about your business, the owner, and your bank. Stripe asks for this by law to verify every business it pays out to.
4. **Add your bank account.** This is where your payouts land.
5. You'll come back to a page that says **"You're ready to take payments."**
   If it says **"Almost there"**, Stripe is reviewing your details. That usually clears within a day, and we'll check on it.

Once you're approved, we turn on the payment section of your website. You don't have to do anything else.

## Day-to-day use

### Get the Stripe app
Search **"Stripe Dashboard"** in the App Store or Google Play and sign in. Payments, invoices and payouts are all on your phone.

### Send an invoice
1. In the app, tap **+ → Invoice**. On a computer, go to **Invoices → Create invoice**.
2. Add the customer's name and email, then the line items, for example "Paver patio, 420 sq ft — balance due."
3. Set **Payment due** (for example, "Due in 7 days").
4. Under payment methods, keep **Card** and **US bank account (ACH)** turned on.
5. Tap **Send**. The customer gets an email with a **Pay** button, and Stripe follows up if it's late.

### Take a deposit
- **Option 1:** send a deposit invoice the same way, for example "50% deposit — Smith backyard."
- **Option 2:** tell the customer to use **"Make a payment"** on your website and enter the job address.

### When does the money arrive?
| Payment | Lands in your bank |
|---|---|
| Card | about **2 business days** after the payment |
| Bank transfer (ACH) | about **4 business days** after the customer pays |

Stripe deposits automatically. You can see every upcoming payout under **Balances**.

### Refunds
Open the payment, then tap **Refund** and choose the full or a partial amount. Stripe doesn't charge a new fee for refunds, but the original processing fee isn't returned.

### If a customer disputes a charge
Stripe emails you. Answer from the Dashboard with your signed estimate, photos of the finished work, and any texts or emails about the job. Keep those for every job; they're your best evidence.

## Good habits

- **Put the scope and price in writing before you start.** Arizona homeowners expect it, and it protects you in a dispute.
- **Use clear invoice descriptions**, like the job address plus what was done, so the charge makes sense on their statement.
- **Keep your Stripe info current.** If you change banks, owners or address, update it under **Settings → Business**.
- **Never share your Stripe password or login codes** with anyone, including us. We'll never ask for them.
